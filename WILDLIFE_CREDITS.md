## Animal-call sounds (played with footage)

8-second tracks in `app/src/main/res/raw/wild_snd_*.ogg`, each = an excerpt of the call recording
(high-passed at 300 Hz, loudness-normalised) mixed with a quiet leaf-rustle bed (excerpt of
"Rustling leaves", Gravity Sound, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rustling_leaves_(Gravity_Sound).wav).
